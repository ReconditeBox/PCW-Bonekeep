BONEKEEP
PLAYER'S MANUAL
===============

(c) 2026 Tony Blews tonyblews@gmail.com


INTRODUCTION
------------

You have entered BONEKEEP, a dungeon of rooms, passages, doors, treasure and
restless dead.

Explore carefully, survive what you find, and search for a way out.


REQUIRED FILES
--------------

BONEKEEP.COM requires BONEKEEP.DAT and the LEVEL.xxx files supplied with
the game. Keep them together in the same directory.


PLAYER NAME
-----------

Before a new game begins, BONEKEEP asks for your name.

Enter from 1 to 30 printable characters and press RETURN.
BACKSPACE may be used to correct the name before starting.


THE SCREEN
----------

The large area on the left shows the part of the dungeon you have explored.

On BONE80.COM this is a scrolling 64 x 18 view of the same 75 x 24
dungeon.  The view follows you automatically as you move.

The panel on the right shows:

    SKILL       your fighting ability
    STAMINA     your remaining health
    GOLD        gold collected
    KEYS        keys carried
    INVENTORY   up to six carried item slots

Your position is shown by @.

Explored parts of the dungeon remain visible after you leave them.


MOVEMENT
--------

Lowercase movement keys move one square:

    w       up
    s       down
    a       left
    d       right

Uppercase movement keys run in a straight line:

    W       run up
    S       run down
    A       run left
    D       run right

Running stops when something prevents normal movement.

Press . to wait in place for one turn.

Four consecutive . turns restore 1 STAMINA, up to your current maximum.
Any other action or any combat interrupts the rest sequence.


OTHER CONTROLS
--------------

    c or C      close an adjacent open door
    o or O      open an adjacent closed or locked door
    l           lock an adjacent closed door
    1 to 6      select inventory slots 1 to 6
    q or Q      quit the game


DOORS AND KEYS
--------------

    +       closed or locked door
    /       open door.

Walking into an ordinary closed door opens it.

A locked door requires a key. Unlocking it consumes one key, and BONEKEEP
asks for confirmation before the key is used.

The o command can open an adjacent door without moving through it.
The c command closes an adjacent open door.
The l command locks an adjacent closed door.


STAIRS
------

> represents stairs going down.
< represents stairs going up.

BONEKEEP asks for confirmation before you use stairs.


GOLD AND KEYS
-------------

$ is gold.
k is a key.

Walking onto gold or a key picks it up automatically.

Gold and keys do not use inventory slots.


PILES OF BONES
--------------

x marks a pile of bones.

Trying to step onto one searches it automatically.

You might find gold. You might find nothing. You might disturb something.


ITEMS
-----

The current item symbols are:

    !       Sword
    *       Shield
    A       Armour
    p       Potion
    f       Food
    K       Bone Crown

When you try to step onto an item, BONEKEEP tells you what it is and asks
whether you want to pick it up.

If you say no, you still move onto the square. The item remains there.

If you say yes, the item is placed in your inventory.

Potions and Food stack.

Drinking a Potion always uses one Potion and one turn. Each Potion has an
equal chance of one of four effects:

    STAMINA     +4 current and maximum STAMINA for 15 turns
    SKILL       +2 SKILL for 15 turns
    INVISIBLE   monsters cannot see or pursue you for 15 turns
    NOTHING     no effect

Only one Potion effect can be active at once. Drinking another Potion replaces
the previous effect. Invisibility does not prevent combat if you walk into a
monster.

Using Food restores some STAMINA, up to your current maximum.
Food is not consumed if you are already at maximum STAMINA.


If the inventory is full, the item remains on the floor.


INVENTORY
---------

The inventory contains six slots, numbered 1 through 6.

Press the slot number to select an item.

The available actions are:

    u       use
    d       drop
    e       equip or unequip, where allowed

Sword, Shield, Armour and the Bone Crown can be equipped.

Equipped items are marked with * in the inventory list.

Skeletons and zombies ignore you while the Bone Crown is equipped.
The vampire remains hostile, but is weaker while separated from the Crown.

Sword, Shield and Armour are shown with a +N value. Higher values indicate
more powerful equipment.

Equipment found deeper in the dungeon becomes more powerful.
Skeletons and Zombies also become stronger as you descend.

Only equipped items give their bonuses.

Swords improve your attacks, Shields can block attacks, and Armour can reduce
damage that gets through.

Equipping another item of the same type automatically unequips the old one.

If you find a better version of an item you already have equipped, BONEKEEP
offers to replace the equipped item. The inferior equipped item is discarded.

Dropping an equipped item automatically unequips it.


COMBAT
------

Skeletons, zombies, vampires and vampire bats block movement.

Trying to move into one starts combat.

Your SKILL and equipment affect how well you fight. Some attacks can become
critical hits.

Keep an eye on your STAMINA. If it reaches zero, you are dead.


SKELETONS
---------

S marks a skeleton.

Skeletons move quickly and will pursue you when they can see you.

Walls, closed doors and other creatures can block them.


ZOMBIES
-------

Zombies hit harder than Skeletons when they land a blow.

Z marks a zombie.

Zombies are slower than skeletons but can still be dangerous once they see you.

Walls, closed doors and other creatures can block them.


VAMPIRES
--------

V marks a vampire.

Vampires are powerful undead and pursue you when they can see you.

The Crown Vampire has a different generated name in each new game.
Vampire transformations and Crown recovery are announced by name.

A badly wounded vampire changes into a bat, shown by the " symbol.

When this happens, the Bone Crown is left behind, shown as K.

The bat flees rapidly while recovering. If it survives long enough, it changes
back into a vampire.


COFFINS
-------

C marks a coffin.

Trying to move into a coffin asks whether you want to open it.

If you decline, you stay where you are.

Opening the coffin wakes the Crown Vampire.


LEAVING THE DUNGEON
-------------------

X is a dungeon exit.

You can only escape while carrying the Bone Crown.

Escaping completes the game and displays a record of your adventure.

TIPS
----

Doors can be useful barriers between you and pursuing undead.

Skeletons are fast. Zombies are slower, but no less worth respecting.

Bone piles are worth searching, but not always safe.

Keys are useful. Do not waste them.


Good luck in BONEKEEP.
